A. THEORETICAL QUESTIONS (3 marks each)

Q5.1: Simple Harmonic Motion Definition [3 marks]

Define simple harmonic motion (SHM). What is the condition for SHM? Derive the equation of motion for SHM.

* Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM) Definition: SHM is periodic motion where the restoring force (or acceleration) is directly proportional to the displacement from equilibrium and always directed toward equilibrium.

Condition for SHM: F = -kx (or a = -ω²x)

Where:

• F is the restoring force
• x is displacement from equilibrium
• k is a constant (spring constant)
• Negative sign indicates force opposes displacement

Equation of Motion Derivation:

Newton's second law: F = ma -kx = m(d²x/dt²) d²x/dt² + (k/m)x = 0

Let ω² = k/m (ω is angular frequency)

d²x/dt² + ω²x = 0 (Differential equation of SHM)

Solution: x(t) = A cos(ωt + φ)

Or: x(t) = A cos(ωt) + B sin(ωt)

Where:

• A is amplitude (maximum displacement)
• ω is angular frequency
• φ is phase constant
• T = 2π/ω is period
• f = ω/2π is frequency

Properties of SHM: 1. Acceleration: a(t) = -ω²A cos(ωt + φ) = -ω²x 2. Velocity: v(t) = -ωA sin(ωt + φ) 3. Maximum velocity: vmax = ωA (at equilibrium) 4. Maximum acceleration: amax = ω²A (at extreme positions) 5. Period is independent of amplitude 6. Energy oscillates between potential and kinetic

Q5.2: Energy in SHM [3 marks]

Show that the total energy in SHM is constant. How are kinetic and potential energies distributed?

* Energy in SHM:

For SHM: x(t) = A cos(ωt) v(t) = dx/dt = -Aω sin(ωt)

Potential Energy: U(t) = ½kx² = ½mω²x² U(t) = ½mω²A² cos²(ωt)

Kinetic Energy: K(t) = ½mv² = ½m(-Aω sin(ωt))² K(t) = ½mω²A² sin²(ωt)

Total Energy: Etotal = K + U Etotal = ½mω²A² sin²(ωt) + ½mω²A² cos²(ωt) Etotal = ½mω²A²[sin²(ωt) + cos²(ωt)] Etotal = ½mω²A² = ½kA² (constant!)

Energy Distribution:

At different positions: 1. At Equilibrium (x = 0):

• U = 0 (all energy is kinetic)
• K = ½mω²A² = Etotal
• v is maximum

2. At Amplitude (x = ±A):

• U = ½kA² = Etotal (all energy is potential)
• K = 0
• v = 0 (momentary rest)

3. At x = A/2:

• U = ½k(A/2)² = ⅛kA²
• K = ½kA² - ⅛kA² = ⅜kA²
• K:U ratio = 3:1

Energy Oscillation Frequency: Eoscillates with twice the frequency of motion (SHM frequency = f, energy oscillation = 2f)

B. NUMERICAL QUESTIONS (5 marks each)

Q5.3: SHM Period and Frequency [5 marks]

A 0.5 kg mass is attached to a spring with spring constant k = 200 N/m. The mass is pulled to 5 cm from equilibrium and released. Find: (a) Period of oscillation (b) Frequency (c) Angular frequency (d) Maximum velocity (e) Maximum acceleration

*

Given: m = 0.5 kg, k = 200 N/m, A = 0.05 m

(a) Period: T = 2π√(m/k) = 2π√(0.5/200) T = 2π√(0.0025) = 2π(0.05) T = 0.314 seconds

(b) Frequency: f = 1/T = 1/0.314 = 3.18 Hz

(c) Angular Frequency: ω = 2πf = 2π(3.18) = 20 rad/s

Or: ω = √(k/m) = √(200/0.5) = √400 = 20 rad/s ✓

(d) Maximum Velocity: vmax = ωA = 20 × 0.05 = 1.0 m/s

Occurs when passing through equilibrium.

(e) Maximum Acceleration: amax = ω²A = 20² × 0.05 = 400 × 0.05 = 20 m/s²

Occurs at maximum displacement (±A).
