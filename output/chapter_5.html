A. THEORETICAL QUESTIONS (3 marks each)

Q5.1: Simple Harmonic Motion Definition [3 marks]

Define simple harmonic motion (SHM). What is the condition for SHM? Derive the equation of motion for SHM.

* > Definition: > Periodic motion where the restoring force is directly proportional to the displacement and acts towards the equilibrium position. > $$ F \propto -x $$

Key Equations:

| Qty | Equation | Condition | | :--- | :--- | :--- | | Force | $F = -kx$ | Hooke's Law | | Motion | $x(t) = A \cos(\omega t + \phi)$ | Position | | Accel | $a = -\omega^2 x$ | Max at Extremes |

---

Q5.2: Energy in SHM [3 marks]

Show that the total energy in SHM is constant. How are kinetic and potential energies distributed?

* > Conservation Principle: > In ideal SHM (no friction), energy continuously cycles between Kinetic (Motion) and Potential (Spring Stored), but the Total Energy ($E$) remains constant.

Energy Distribution Table:

| Position | Displacement ($x$) | Kinetic Energy ($K$) | Potential Energy ($U$) | | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | | Mean | $0$ | Max ($\frac{1}{2}kA^2$) | Zero ($0$) | | Extreme | $\pm A$ | Zero ($0$) | Max ($\frac{1}{2}kA^2$) | | Any | $x$ | $\frac{1}{2}k(A^2-x^2)$ | $\frac{1}{2}kx^2$ |

Visual Equation: $$ E_{total} = K + U = \frac{1}{2}kA^2 = \text{Constant} $$

B. NUMERICAL QUESTIONS (5 marks each)

Q5.3: SHM Period and Frequency [5 marks]

A 0.5 kg mass is attached to a spring with spring constant k = 200 N/m. The mass is pulled to 5 cm from equilibrium and released. Find: (a) Period of oscillation (b) Frequency (c) Angular frequency (d) Maximum velocity (e) Maximum acceleration

* Concept: * Period (T): How many seconds for *one full round trip?* (Depends on how heavy the mass is and how stiff the spring is). * Frequency (f): How many round trips *per second?* (Opposite of Period). * Amplitude (A): How far does it stretch?

Formula: * T = 2π√(m/k) * ω = 2π/T * vmax = ωA * amax = ω²A

Calculation: Given: m=0.5, k=200, A=0.05.

(a) Period: T = 2π√(0.5/200) = 2π√(0.0025) = 2π(0.05) T = 0.314 s

(b) Frequency: f = 1/T = 3.18 Hz

(c) Angular Freq: ω = 200/0.5 = √400 = 20 rad/s

(d) Max Velocity: vmax = 20(0.05) = 1 m/s

(e) Max Acceleration: amax = 20²(0.05) = 400(0.05) = 20 m/s²
