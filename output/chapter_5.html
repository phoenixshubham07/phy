A. THEORETICAL QUESTIONS (3 marks each)

Q5.1: Simple Harmonic Motion Definition [3 marks]

Define simple harmonic motion (SHM). What is the condition for SHM? Derive the equation of motion for SHM.

* Concept: SHM is periodic motion where restoring force is proportional to displacement and directed towards equilibrium.

Formula: * Force: F = -kx * Differential Equation: d²x/dt² + ω²x = 0 * Solution: x(t) = A cos(ωt + φ)

Key Properties: 1. Acceleration: a = -ω²x (Max at extremes) 2. Velocity: v = ±ω√(A²-x²) (Max at center) 3. Period: Independent of Amplitude.

---

Q5.2: Energy in SHM [3 marks]

Show that the total energy in SHM is constant. How are kinetic and potential energies distributed?

* Concept: Total Mechanical Energy (E) in SHM is conserved. It oscillates between Kinetic (K) and Potential (U) forms.

Formula: * U = ½kx² = ½mω²x² * K = ½mv² = ½mω²(A²-x²) * Etotal = K + U = ½kA²

Energy Distribution: * Equilibrium (x=0): Max K, Zero U. * Extremes (x=±A): Max U, Zero K.

B. NUMERICAL QUESTIONS (5 marks each)

Q5.3: SHM Period and Frequency [5 marks]

A 0.5 kg mass is attached to a spring with spring constant k = 200 N/m. The mass is pulled to 5 cm from equilibrium and released. Find: (a) Period of oscillation (b) Frequency (c) Angular frequency (d) Maximum velocity (e) Maximum acceleration

* Concept: Period depends on mass and stiffness (k). Velocity and acceleration depend on amplitude and frequency.

Formula: * T = 2π√(m/k) * ω = 2π/T * vmax = ωA * amax = ω²A

Calculation: Given: m=0.5, k=200, A=0.05.

(a) Period: T = 2π√(0.5/200) = 2π√(0.0025) = 2π(0.05) T = 0.314 s

(b) Frequency: f = 1/T = 3.18 Hz

(c) Angular Freq: ω = 200/0.5 = √400 = 20 rad/s

(d) Max Velocity: vmax = 20(0.05) = 1 m/s

(e) Max Acceleration: amax = 20²(0.05) = 400(0.05) = 20 m/s²
