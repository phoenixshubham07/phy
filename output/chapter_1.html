A. THEORETICAL QUESTIONS (3 marks each)

Q1.1: Scalars vs Vectors [3 marks]

Define scalar and vector quantities. Give three examples of each and explain why position is a vector but distance is a scalar.

* Scalars: Magnitude only. * Examples: Mass, Speed, Energy.

Vectors: Magnitude + Direction. * Examples: Velocity, Force, Displacement.

Position vs Distance: * Distance: Total path covered (Scalar). * Position: Location relative to origin with direction (Vector).

---

Q1.2: Vector Addition Methods [3 marks]

Explain the triangle law and parallelogram law of vector addition. When would you use each method?

* 1. Triangle Law: * Use when: Vectors are sequential (head-to-tail). * Statement: If two vectors are sides of a triangle, resultant is the third side (start to end). * Formula: |R| = √(A² + B² + 2AB cosθ)

2. Parallelogram Law: * Use when: Vectors act simultaneously from one point (tail-to-tail). * Statement: Resultant is the diagonal of the parallelogram formed by the two vectors.

---

Q1.3: Dot Product Applications [3 marks]

What is the dot product of two vectors? Show how it's used to find the angle between them and explain why A·B = 0 means perpendicular vectors.

* Definition: A · B = |A||B| cosθ (Product of magnitudes and cosine of angle).

Finding Angle: * cosθ = (A · B) / (|A| |B|)

Perpendicular Condition: * If A · B = 0, then cosθ = 0 → θ = 90°. * Zero dot product = Perpendicular vectors.

Q1.4: Relative Velocity Concept [3 marks]

Define relative velocity. A boat moves at 10 m/s in still water. If the river current is 4 m/s, what does it mean when we say the boat's velocity relative to the ground? Draw a velocity diagram.

* Relative Velocity: Velocity of an object observed from a moving frame.

Boat Example: * vboat = 10 m/s (in water) * vcurrent = 4 m/s (river) * Resultant = Vector sum of boat speed + current.

Diagram: 10 m/s (Boat) ↑ | ________|____→ 4 m/s (Current) / / Resultant /

Meaning: An observer on the bank sees the boat move diagonally at the resultant speed.

B. NUMERICAL QUESTIONS (5 marks each)

Q1.5: Resultant of Two Vectors [5 marks]

Two forces of 12 N and 16 N act on a point at an angle of 60° to each other. Find (a) the magnitude of the resultant, (b) the angle it makes with the 12 N force, and (c) the angle it makes with the 16 N force.

* Given: F₁ = 12 N, F₂ = 16 N, θ = 60°

(a) Magnitude of Resultant: R = √(F₁² + F₂² + 2F₁F₂ cosθ) R = √(12² + 16² + 2(12)(16) cos60°) R = √(144 + 256 + 2(12)(16)(0.5)) R = √(144 + 256 + 192) R = √592 = 24.33 N

(b) Angle with 12 N force (α): Using sine rule: sin(α)/F₂ = sin(θ)/R sin(α) = F₂ sin(θ) / R sin(α) = 16 × sin(60°) / 24.33 sin(α) = 16 × 0.866 / 24.33 sin(α) = 13.856 / 24.33 = 0.569 α = 34.7°

(c) Angle with 16 N force (β): β = θ - α = 60° - 34.7° = 25.3°

Or using: sin(β)/F₁ = sin(θ)/R sin(β) = 12 × sin(60°) / 24.33 = 0.427 β = 25.3°

Q1.6: River-Boat Problem [5 marks]

A boat capable of moving at 8 m/s in still water wants to cross a river of width 240 m. The current flows at 6 m/s. (a) In what direction should the boat head to cross perpendicular to the banks? (b) How long will it take to cross? (c) What is the resultant velocity of the boat?

*

(a) Direction to head perpendicular to banks: To go perpendicular, the boat's velocity component parallel to the current must equal and opposite to the current.

Let θ be the angle upstream from perpendicular.

• Perpendicular component needed: v_⊥ = √(8² - 6²) = √(64 - 36) = √28 = 5.29 m/s
• Parallel component (upstream): v_∥ = 6 m/s

sin(θ) = 6/8 = 0.75 θ = 48.6° upstream from perpendicular (or 41.4° from the bank)

(b) Time to cross: The perpendicular component carries the boat across: t = width / v_⊥ = 240 / 5.29 = 45.4 seconds

(c) Resultant velocity: Since boat crosses perpendicular, resultant velocity = perpendicular component = 5.29 m/s perpendicular to banks

(The parallel components cancel, so the boat moves straight across)

Q1.7: Relative Velocity - Two Objects [5 marks]

A car A travels due East at 60 km/h. Car B travels due North at 80 km/h. Both pass through the same point. Find the velocity of car B relative to car A and the angle it makes.

*

Given:

• vA = 60 km/h due East
• vB = 80 km/h due North

Velocity of B relative to A: vBA = vB - vA

In component form:

• vA = 60i + 0j km/h
• vB = 0i + 80j km/h
• vBA = (0-60)i + (80-0)j = -60i + 80j km/h

Magnitude: |vBA| = √((-60)² + 80²) = √(3600 + 6400) = √10000 = 100 km/h

Direction: The velocity of B relative to A makes an angle with the negative x-axis (West direction): tan(φ) = 80/60 = 4/3 φ = 53.1° North of West (or 126.9° from East)

Interpretation: From car A's perspective, car B appears to move at 100 km/h in a direction 53.1° North of West.

Q1.8: Cross Product & Projection [5 marks]

Given vectors A = 3i + 4j and B = i + j. Find: (a) The cross product A × B. (b) The projection of A on B.

* (a) Cross Product: A × B = (3i + 4j) × (i + j) = 3(i×i) + 3(i×j) + 4(j×i) + 4(j×j) = 0 + 3(k) + 4(-k) + 0 = -k

(b) Projection of A on B: Proj = (A·B) / |B| A·B = (3)(1) + (4)(1) = 7 |B| = √(1² + 1²) = √2 Projection = 7/√2 = 4.95
